About

You Figure It Out

The name is a joke about the answer venues usually get. Our whole business is being the people who give a different one.

Who we are

YFIO LLC is a Colorado company. We provide vendoring and merchandising services to venues — music halls, breweries, festivals, theaters, and event spaces — across the state.

Vending and merch are the parts of an event that get handed off last and questioned first. Somebody has to source the product, staff the booth, count it twice, and settle up at the end of the night. We do that work, and we do it in a way you can audit.

How we operate

  • Show up prepared. Product, staff, and setup ready before doors.
  • Count honestly. In and out, every event, no exceptions.
  • Fit the venue. Your rules, your room, your crowd.
  • Be reachable. One point of contact who answers.
