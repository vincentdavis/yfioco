Services

What YFIO handles

Pick the whole program or just the piece you are missing. Most venues start with one event and grow from there.

Vendoring

We source and stock the product, get it to the venue, set up the point of sale, and staff it. Concessions, retail product, specialty goods — matched to your crowd and your license.

Merchandising

Merch booth design, display and pricing, restock during the show, count-in and count-out, and settlement with the artist or the promoter at the end of the night.

Inventory management

Counts you can trust. We track what came in, what sold, and what is left, and hand the numbers back in a format you can actually use.

Event staffing

Trained sellers who know the product and the room. We cover setup, the run of show, and teardown so your staff is not stretched across two jobs.

Sourcing & supplier coordination

Finding the right product at the right margin, and dealing with the suppliers, lead times, and reorders so you do not have to.

Sales tax & licensing

We collect and remit the local sales tax on every sale, and carry the vendor licenses it requires. Colorado's home-rule cities each administer their own tax, so we file to whichever jurisdiction the event falls under.

Reporting & settlement

Post-event reporting on sales, inventory, and settlement, with the detail you need for your books and for your artist and vendor agreements.

How an engagement works

  1. Walkthrough

    We look at the space, the calendar, and the crowd, and talk through what has and has not worked before.

  2. Proposal

    You get a written plan: product mix, staffing, setup, terms, and what the split looks like.

  3. Run the event

    We handle sourcing, delivery, setup, sales, and teardown. Your team runs the venue.

  4. Settle and review

    Numbers and settlement come back after the event, the sales tax gets filed, and we tell you what we would change next time.

Not sure which piece you need?

Most venues call us because something specific is breaking — merch is a mess, a vendor fell through, or nobody wants to own the counts. Start there and we will scope the rest around it.
