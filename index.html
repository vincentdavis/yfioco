What we do

Three services that cover the full path from product on a shelf to money in the register.

Vendoring

Sourcing, stocking, and running vendor operations on site — product selection, delivery, setup, and staffing through the length of the event.

Merchandising

Merch programs built for your venue and your acts — display, pricing, restock, settlement, and a booth that actually looks like it belongs there.

Reporting & settlement

Clear counts, clean numbers. You get sales, inventory, and settlement detail after every event instead of a shoebox of receipts.
